# Community Code of Conduct

We want a welcoming, useful space for people with different levels of experience and perspectives. Treat others with respect, discuss ideas rather than attacking people, and make room for questions and good-faith corrections.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, publishing someone's private information, and pressuring people to share personal or medical details. Critique claims with evidence and care; no contributor is expected to disclose personal experience.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, warn a participant, pause participation, or remove someone from the project. They will consider context and aim for a proportionate response.

Report a concern privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub contact options. Do not post personal details or incident evidence in a public issue. Maintainers will restrict access to a report to people who need it to respond.
