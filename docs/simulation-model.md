# Simulation model

## Profiles and situations

The app provides ten fictional profiles with different care-planning themes. They are scenario prompts, not breed descriptions or claims about a real dog. A profile must not imply that size or a single personality label determines an individual animal's needs.

Seven daily prompts cover schedules, cost planning, walks and play, basic care, shared responsibilities, an unexpected situation, and reflection. Responses are recorded as practice choices and linked to suggestions in the report.

## Design principles

- Use profiles to open questions, not to classify real dogs.
- Present uncertainty and individual variation plainly.
- Prefer practical preparation steps to warnings or judgments.
- Keep fictional scenarios distinct from real-user or expert evidence.
- Do not turn general learning content into diagnosis, treatment, or legal advice.

## Changes that need review

Changes to animal-health or behavior situations, profile framing, score effects, or user-facing learning cards require an issue/spec and maintainer review. Content that makes an expert claim also needs an appropriately qualified reviewer before it is described as expert-reviewed.
