# Safety and responsibility

The app is a preparation exercise. Its score and suggestions summarize a short simulation; they do not decide whether someone should adopt or predict what a real dog will do.

## User-facing boundaries

- Keep the simulation limits near the report and score.
- Do not imply veterinary diagnosis, treatment, behavior diagnosis, or legal advice.
- Encourage users to discuss practical plans with household members and to ask shelters, veterinarians, qualified behavior professionals, or relevant local authorities when appropriate.
- Avoid language that shames users or treats an incomplete record as failure.

## Maintainer gate

Any change to animal health, behavior, training, breed or personality framing, scoring, privacy, data retention, camera use, or safety copy requires an issue/spec and maintainer approval. When a claim depends on specialist knowledge, require review by a qualified professional before describing it as reviewed guidance.

Tests can show that rules are deterministic and that safety copy remains present. They do not validate the rules against real adoption outcomes or establish professional consensus.
