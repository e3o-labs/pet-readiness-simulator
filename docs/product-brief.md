# Product brief

## Purpose

Pet Readiness Simulator is a seven-day practice app for people considering life with a dog. It turns everyday care situations into prompts for planning routines, support, and questions to ask before making a decision.

## User need

People may know that they like dogs but have not yet planned how recurring care fits with their time, household, housing, costs, and support. The app helps make those questions concrete.

## Product boundary

The app uses fictional profiles and a deterministic simulation. It does not assess whether a person qualifies to adopt, predict an individual dog's behavior, or replace advice from shelters, veterinarians, qualified behavior professionals, or local authorities. Its score is a reflection aid, not a validated outcome measure.

## Core experience

1. Describe a few current life conditions.
2. Choose a fictional profile to practice with.
3. Respond to care prompts and situations over seven days.
4. Record brief reflections and review preparation questions.

The app is designed to keep the user in control: skipped or missing records remain visible, and the result should lead to a next question rather than a pass/fail verdict.
