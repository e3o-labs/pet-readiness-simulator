# Risk and responsibility

## Overclaiming

The app must not say that someone can or cannot adopt, predict an individual animal, or present a rule-based score as scientifically validated. Keep the score's limits beside the result.

## Stereotyping

Fictional profiles must invite observation and planning. Avoid claims that a breed, size, or single trait determines behavior or suitability.

## Shame and distress

Low or incomplete scores should lead to practical next steps, not blame, urgency, or pressure to abandon or adopt.

## Animal health and behavior

Do not provide diagnosis or individualized treatment instructions. Escalate medical, emergency, severe fear, aggression, and sudden behavior-change content to qualified professionals.

## Privacy and media

Do not commit user notes, feedback exports, photographs, contact details, or device evidence. The optional feedback flow requires an operator-managed endpoint and public privacy notice. Camera features remain experimental and off by default.

## Evidence integrity

Synthetic test data proves only that a code path behaves as tested. It does not prove user benefit, market demand, scientific validity, or real-world animal outcomes. Clearly label synthetic fixtures and keep real operational evidence outside this repository.

## Third-party rights

Use only assets with a recorded source and a license or permission that allows the intended redistribution. Exclude competitor screenshots and unverified generated or user-submitted media.
