# Seven source-reviewed lessons

The web app includes seven lessons with three project-generated scene illustrations each. The header education menu and daily learning tab share the same article. Each article includes a short explanation, one action and a reflection question. Days 1–6 link to official source material; day 7 is a product reflection activity. Source checks are dated 2026-10-21. External expert approval is not a prerequisite for these bounded summaries; they do not claim expert endorsement, clinical validation, diagnostic accuracy or prediction of adoption outcomes.

| Day | Topic | Source basis |
|---|---|---|
|1|Alone time and alternative care|RSPCA separation-related behaviour and gradual alone training|
|2|Food and care spending|WSAVA treats guidance; budgeting is a reflection activity without a prescribed amount|
|3|Comfortable distance on walks|Dogs Trust body language/context guidance|
|4|Responses before handling|Dogs Trust gradual positive handling guidance|
|5|Consistent family signals|VCA predictability, communication and reward guidance|
|6|Context before responding to barking|AVSAB humane training, VCA alternatives, Dogs Trust distance/context|
|7|One practical change before adoption|Product reflection, no clinical claim|

Full source URLs are in each card and open from the article. Institutional photos and diagrams are not copied. The own illustrations are context examples, not pose-based diagnoses or installation instructions. No numeric feeding prescription, treatment protocol, fixed alone-time threshold or scoring rule is imported.

Public synchronization contains only these reviewed cards, shared UI, 21 illustrations and public documentation. Native releases, operator records, historical research registers and participant data are excluded. Existing public feedback intake and data handling are unchanged.
